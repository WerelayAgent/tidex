Providing liquidity on Uniswap pays well, as long as someone keeps an eye on it. That someone is now Tidex Fi.

Doing it yourself

  • The price leaves your range and your position stops earning, often without you noticing.
  • Moving it back means watching charts, withdrawing, swapping and re-depositing by hand.
  • Earned fees sit idle until you claim and re-add them yourself.

With Tidex Fi

  • Your range is moved back around the price automatically, day and night.
  • Fees are reinvested for you, so what you earn starts earning too.
  • Set it up once in about a minute. Claim or withdraw whenever you like.
