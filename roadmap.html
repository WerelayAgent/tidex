Roadmap

What is built, what comes next, and where Tidex Fi is heading.

  1. Phase 1

    Building

    Done
    • App design and website (done)
    • Whitepaper (done)
    • Smart contracts (done)
    • Automation for ranges and fees (done)
    • Testing on Robinhood Chain testnet (done)
    • Markets page and in-app swap (done)
    • Security audit (done)
  2. Phase 2

    Launch

    Now
    • App live on Robinhood Chain (done)
    • TIDEX launch on PonsUp next
    • Staking with fee discounts
    • Staking rewards paid in ETH
    • Buyback and burn from launch day
  3. Phase 3

    Growth

    After launch
    • Listing on CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap
    • Tidex Fi lending: earn rewards by lending USDG and other tokens to leveraged positions
    • Leveraged liquidity, backed by your Tidex Fi position
    • Boosted pools: extra rewards on selected pools
    • Liquidity shapes: choose how a position spreads its liquidity over the range (even, centred or more at the edges), managed as one position
    • New position types, such as selling at a target price
  4. Phase 4

    Beyond

    Later
    • Tidex Fi mobile app for iOS and Android: follow and manage your positions on the go
    • Other chains

Have an idea or a wish? Tell us in the community, the roadmap grows with the people who use Tidex Fi.
